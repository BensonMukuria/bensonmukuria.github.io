About me

I am a data analyst who likes the whole job: getting the data in shape, building a clean model, and then making a report that a busy person can read in a minute.

My work focuses on spotting what the numbers are saying, such as where growth is coming from, which stores are slipping, and when the business is busiest, and then turning that into clear next steps.
