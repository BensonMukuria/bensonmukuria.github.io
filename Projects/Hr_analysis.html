1,470employees analyzed across the organization

Introduction

This HR analysis report provides an overview of key factors related to diversity and inclusion, employee well-being, distribution across job levels, and the identification of employees due for promotion within the organization. The assessment primarily focuses on gender distribution as a measure of diversity and inclusion, while considering employee satisfaction in terms of marital status, work environment, and work-life balance.

By examining these aspects, the report provides insights into the current state of the organization and highlights areas that require attention. These findings serve as a foundation for strategies that foster a more inclusive and supportive work environment, enhance employee well-being, and ensure fair opportunities for career growth.

Problem statement

The organization faces challenges related to diversity and inclusion, employee well-being, distribution across job levels, and the identification of employees due for promotion. These issues require attention to ensure an inclusive workforce, enhance employee satisfaction, address potential disparities in career advancement, and establish a streamlined process for recognizing and promoting deserving employees.

Data sourcing

The organization provided a comprehensive dataset in CSV format for this analysis. It includes employee demographics, satisfaction ratings on the work environment, marital status, and work-life balance, along with information on job levels and promotion eligibility. This dataset serves as the foundation for deriving insights and recommendations.

Data transformation

The dataset underwent transformation to improve its usability. One key step converted satisfaction ratings into two categories — "satisfied" or "unsatisfied" (and "good" or "bad") — using additional columns built in Power Query. This enabled a more direct read on employee satisfaction and well-being.

Recommendations

Enhancing diversity and inclusion

Address the gender imbalance through targeted recruitment, diversity training, and a work environment that visibly values and celebrates diversity.

Improving employee well-being

Provide resources and support systems for work-life balance, and continuously assess the work environment to sustain satisfaction and engagement.

Recognizing long-serving employees

Formalize a recognition program for long-tenured employees, using skills assessment, mentoring, and personalized development plans.

Continuous data analysis

Regularly monitor HR data to catch emerging trends and disparities early, and use the findings to drive targeted interventions.
